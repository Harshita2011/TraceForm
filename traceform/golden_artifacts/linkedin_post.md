## Hook
Most SaaS founders think churn is a product problem. It almost never is.

## Story
Last quarter, an enterprise software leader watched their churn more than double from 4% to 9%, right as top-of-funnel acquisition hit all-time records.

The executive instinct? Blame competitor pricing and demand product feature overhauls.

When we audited the cohort data, the truth was startling:
• 72% of cancellations happened in days 30 to 90.
• The average customer spent 48 days just waiting on API credentials.
• Product satisfaction among customers who completed setup was 94%.

Customers weren't leaving because the product failed. They were leaving because time-to-value took longer than their internal patience.

## Insight
Acquisition without rapid onboarding velocity isn't growth—it's an expensive treadmill. If your time-to-value exceeds 21 days, you are funding pipeline for your competitors.

## Takeaway
1. Measure Time-to-First-Value (TTFV) as aggressively as CAC payback.
2. Automate technical handover before signing new enterprise logos.
3. Align sales compensation with 12-month retention, not first-day signatures.
