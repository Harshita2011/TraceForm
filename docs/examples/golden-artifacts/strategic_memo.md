## Situation
The corporation faces an acute strategic inflection point in enterprise customer retention. While top-of-funnel customer acquisition accelerated 34% YoY to $8.4M in gross new ARR, annualized churn increased from 4% to 9%, eroding $3.8M in baseline revenue and dampening overall enterprise valuation multiples.

## Assessment
Empirical analysis of the Q3 customer cohort reveals that 72% of attrition is concentrated within enterprise accounts (ARR >$100k) during the initial 90 days of contract tenure. The structural bottleneck is technical onboarding latency: average implementation spans 48 calendar days against the contracted 21-day service level agreement, leaving client technical teams unintegrated at initial milestone reviews.

## Implications
1. **Capital Allocation Distortion:** Marketing CAC payback has expanded to 22 months, rendering aggressive top-of-funnel ad spend margin-dilutive until onboarding throughput is resolved.
2. **Enterprise Renewal Vulnerability:** $14.5M in ARR reaches contractual renewal in Q4. Failing to stabilize implementation will compound gross churn to an estimated 12.5%.
3. **Competitive Displacement:** Two primary competitors have introduced zero-code migration accelerators, directly targeting delayed implementations.

## Risks
- **Execution Risk:** Solutions engineering team utilization stands at 94%, creating severe single-point failure liabilities.
- **Contractual Liabilities:** Prolonged SLA breaches expose the firm to clawback penalties across 8 key financial services accounts.

## Strategic Options
- **Option A (Aggressive Engineering Reallocation):** Reallocate $1.2M from Q4 top-of-funnel marketing to customer engineering, recruiting 6 integration engineers and deploying automated API onboarding accelerators.
- **Option B (Partner Channel Outsourcing):** Outsource all Tier-2 customer onboarding to certified systems integrator partners, preserving internal R&D capacity at higher variable unit cost.

## Recommendation
The Strategy Committee unanimously recommends **Option A**. Internalizing the integration architecture ensures long-term IP ownership and reduces median onboarding time to 18 days, securing $14.5M in Q4 renewals with a projected 4.2x ROI over 12 months.
