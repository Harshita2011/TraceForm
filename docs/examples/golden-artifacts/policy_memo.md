## Issue
The governance committee must establish an enterprise policy governing the operational deployment of generative AI across internal decision-making and external communications to mitigate hallucination liability and intellectual property leakage.

## Context
Recent operational expansions into regulated financial and healthcare verticals require strict adherence to SOC2 Type II, ISO 27001, and emerging AI accountability frameworks. Current ad-hoc employee usage of consumer LLMs creates unacceptable compliance exposures.

## Policy Options
- **Option 1 (Centralized Auditable Intelligence Gateway):** Mandate all internal synthesis and external content drafting route through a single audited platform with mandatory coordinate-level citation verification and automatic PII masking.
- **Option 2 (Decentralized Departmental Guidelines):** Issue guidelines permitting individual business units to select tooling subject to quarterly compliance audits.

## Tradeoffs
Option 1 incurs initial migration overhead ($180k) but guarantees 100% compliance traceability and zero data leakage to public models. Option 2 offers immediate adoption flexibility but introduces recurring audit overhead ($320k annually) and unmitigated regulatory risk.

## Recommendation
The Governance Committee recommends immediate adoption of **Option 1**, establishing a mandatory coordinate-verified intelligence architecture across all departments effective Q4 2026.
